Every metric on this screen is now a self-contained circular card (buildCircleCard()) - no rectangular info/viz tile left. Humidity, UV Index, and Pressure all share the exact same ringGauge() function, just at different sizes/sweeps/colors: UV Index's ring and icon are color-coded to the Dashboard's own existing teal/brass/ember UV severity scale (moderate ≥3, high ≥6), Humidity's stays a fixed teal, Pressure's a fixed brass since it has no severity scale. Wind gave up its compass-dial visualization for the same speed ring the others use - a dial's needle pivots from the card's own center, which collided with the centered label/value/unit text every *other* gauge here leaves room for by being hollow in the middle; direction stays as text ("From NW") in the unit line instead. Visibility gets a scalloped disc (scallopPath(), a cosine-modulated radius computed the same way every other curve on this screen is, not a fixed decorative path). Air Quality keeps its single-number-plus-scale panel (AQI + category + dominant pollutant), left as-is; Pollen gets its own pill-shaped panel with a ring gauge per type (Grass/Tree/Weed) - a real 0–5 Universal Pollen Index, not the 0–4 scale a certain phone weather app's own pollen cards use. Both come from separate APIs not yet wired into src/weather.cpp (see environmental-apis.md). Pressure/Visibility/Dew Point/Heat Index/Wind Chill are all already returned by the Weather API's own currentConditions:lookup (just not read by weather_parse.h's filter yet) — the closest thing to a "free" addition here. The card next to Visibility picks Heat Index or Wind Chill dynamically (feelsLikeAdjustment()), not a hardcoded one: NWS's own low-temperature heat index formula (valid at any temperature, not just extreme heat - the full Rothfusz regression only applies 80–112°F) covers everything above 50°F, and wind chill takes over below that when there's at least light wind (≥3 mph), matching NWS's own applicability rule for when wind chill is even defined - full derivation and sourcing in feels-like-formulas.md.
