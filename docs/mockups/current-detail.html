Reached by tapping the Dashboard's own "now" reading (icon/temp/condition), same drill-down affordance as Hourly and 7-Day Outlook. Surfaces everything the Dashboard's compact 4-metric grid leaves out: pressure, visibility, dew point, plus air quality and pollen — two data points Google's Weather API doesn't provide at all, sourced from separate Air Quality and Pollen APIs (see environmental-apis.md for the research behind this, including why those two aren't implemented in firmware yet).

Instrument tiles reuse the Dashboard's .metric severity-color convention (teal/brass/ember, low→high) for UV Index; Air Quality and Pollen get their own panels rather than tiles — both are richer than a single number (AQI + category + dominant pollutant; three pollen types each with their own 0–5 index) and come from entirely separate APIs not yet wired into src/weather.cpp (see environmental-apis.md). Pressure/Visibility/Dew Point are already returned by the Weather API's own currentConditions:lookup (just not read by weather_parse.h's filter yet) — the closest thing to a "free" addition here.
